Why student artists?

All campus art should be created by Duke's own students rather than outsourced to outside contractors. Students live, study and compete on this campus every day, and their work can reflect that experience firsthand. Commissioning student artists keeps the investment within the Duke community and gives students a lasting way to contribute to the spaces they share.
