Possible Proposals

Possible subjects

  • A Duke football game
  • A Duke basketball game
  • Other Duke sports
  • The student body
  • Academic learning in progress, such as labs and classrooms
  • Duke's campuses and well-known spots
  • Students having lunch and enjoying their recreational time
  • Portraits of the Blue Devil

And many more.

Possible locations

The paintings could be displayed in spaces across Duke's campus, such as:

  • Bryan Center
  • Brodhead Center
  • Rubenstein Arts Center
  • Levine Science Research Center (LSRC)
  • Perkins and Bostock Libraries
  • East Campus housing
  • West Campus housing
  • Classroom buildings
  • Card Gym locker room hallway
  • Cameron Indoor Stadium
  • Wilson Recreation Center
  • East Campus Marketplace

And many more.
