The proposal

Twelve paintings

Twelve oil paintings in four sizes.

2

14 × 10 ft

Oil painting

  • A Duke football game
  • A Duke basketball game
4

5 × 7 ft

Oil painting

  • Other Duke sports
  • The student body
  • Academic learning in progress — for example a robotics lab, a chemistry lab or an economics classroom
2

3.5 × 2.5 ft

Oil painting

  • Subjects in the same spirit, such as Duke's campuses and well-known spots
  • Students having lunch and enjoying their recreational time
4

1.25 × 1.75 ft

Oil painting

  • Portraits of the Blue Devil — possibly four different versions
  • Or other subjects, chosen by the public

Possible locations

The original paintings could be displayed in spaces across Duke's campus, such as:

  • Bryan Center
  • Brodhead Center
  • Rubenstein Arts Center
  • Levine Science Research Center (LSRC)
  • Perkins and Bostock Libraries
  • East Campus housing
  • West Campus housing
  • Classroom buildings
  • Card Gym locker room hallway
  • Cameron Indoor Stadium
  • Wilson Recreation Center
  • East Campus Marketplace

And other high-traffic spaces on East and West Campus.

Why a student artist

Major campus art should be created by Duke's own students rather than outsourced to outside contractors.
